# The hero — every animation

The hero: a small hooded wanderer with a pale horned mask, a long dark-blue cloak that trails behind, thin limbs, and a slim curved nail-blade. Pale mask, deep blue cloak, a single teal glow in the eyes and on the blade.

Redo or extend any row. The counts are what the game uses today; more frames are welcome (up to double).

## Prompt (one sheet per row, or all rows on one sheet)

```
Dark hand-painted 2D game art for a side-scrolling action-exploration game: moody, slightly desaturated palette with one glowing accent colour, clean thin dark ink outline of even weight, soft painted shading from a single light source at the top-left, rim light on the edge facing the light, readable silhouette at small size. Side view, character faces RIGHT. Original design, not a copy of any existing game or character.
The hero: a small hooded wanderer with a pale horned mask, a long dark-blue cloak that trails behind, thin limbs, and a slim curved nail-blade. Pale mask, deep blue cloak, a single teal glow in the eyes and on the blade. Animation sheet, frames left to right, one row per animation.
Row "idle" (6 frames now): standing, breathing, cloak edge and a little hood sway; the loop must be seamless.
Row "run" (9 frames now): full run cycle, leaning forward, cloak streaming back, clear contact / passing / push-off poses, feet hit the same ground line.
Row "jump" (6 frames now): take-off crouch, rising stretch, apex tuck, falling with cloak lifted, landing squash (6 poses in order).
Row "slash1" (5 frames now): first nail slash: wind-up, fast swing, follow-through; swing arc drawn as a separate crescent trail in the same cell.
Row "slash2" (5 frames now): second slash coming back the other way (mirror of the first in motion), different body lean.
Row "hit" (4 frames now): struck: flinch, head snapped back, arms flung, regaining balance.
Row "death" (8 frames now): falls, the mask cracks and splits, slumps to the ground, body fades to a pale husk (8 poses, slow).
Row "sit" (2 frames now): sits on a stone bench, resting, cloak spread; 2 poses of slow breathing.
Row "up" (5 frames now): looks and strikes upward: nail raised overhead.
Row "down" (4 frames now): plunging nail-down strike in the air, body inverted slightly, blade pointing at the ground.
Row "wall" (2 frames now): clinging to a wall with one hand and both feet, cloak hanging down.
Row "dash" (2 frames now): a sideways dash: stretched low body and a long cloak streak (2 poses).
Row "double" (4 frames now): double jump: a burst of pale light under the feet, body twisting in the air.
Row "focus" (4 frames now): gathering soul to heal: hands to the chest, light drawn into the body, cloak swirling up.
Row "cast" (4 frames now): casting a soul spell forward: palm thrust out, a glowing orb leaving the hand.
Row "rest" (4 frames now): waking / resting animation after a bench: stretching up, shaking the cloak.
Also draw the nail-slash crescents (3 sizes) and a small dust puff (4 frames) in two extra rows.
One image. A plain flat mid-grey background (#808080), perfectly even: no gradient, no floor, no cast shadow, no vignette, no text, no labels, no frame lines, no watermark. Every drawing sits in its own invisible cell of a regular grid with clear empty grey space around it, never touching or overlapping another. All drawings in the sheet are the SAME SCALE (the body never gets bigger or smaller between frames) and share the same ground line: feet / lowest point of the body on the same baseline in each row. Same character, same colours, same proportions in every frame. High resolution (at least 2048 px wide).
```
